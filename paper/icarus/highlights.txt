Highlights
- Rotation periods for 41,842 asteroids from Gaia DR3; 6,238 with high confidence
- Confidence set by null tests; 89% correct on held-out known periods
- Spin senses agree with published poles for 94-98% of asteroids
- Spin senses reproduce the Yarkovsky segregation in asteroid families
- 108 disputed LCDB periods and 861 candidate new periods are listed
