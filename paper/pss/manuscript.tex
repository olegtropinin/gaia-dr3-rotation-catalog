\title{Rotation periods and spin senses from Gaia DR3 sparse photometry: tested reliability, 790 candidate new periods and disputed LCDB entries}

\author{Oleg Tropynin}
\ead{tropininoleg@gmail.com}
\address{Independent researcher, Karaganda, Kazakhstan}

\begin{abstract}
The error rate of Gaia DR3 sparse-photometry periods for asteroids without a published period has not
been measured. I derive periods and spin senses for numbered asteroids with at
least 25 Gaia DR3 transits, fitting season offsets, a linear phase term and a second-order Fourier series
with the phase corrected for the phase-angle bisector under both senses of rotation. A probability that
each period is correct is calibrated on half of the LCDB and tested on the other half, and the confidence
threshold is set by null tests that keep the Gaia sampling. 6{,}238 periods reach the confident tier;
1{,}680 are absent from the three earlier DR3 analyses. On the test half, 89.3\% are correct within 3\%
(95.4\% where the reference quality is $U\ge3-$); overall, 92.5\% agree with independent ATLAS periods. Spin
senses agree with independent ATLAS poles for 96.4\%, mainly for poles far from the ecliptic; 1{,}447 are for asteroids with no pole in six pole catalogues. They reproduce the known Yarkovsky segregation in families: 74.8\% of
2{,}345 members lie on the predicted side, against $52.4\pm0.9\%$ for permuted senses. 39 LCDB periods
disagree with independent survey data and 69 more are flagged by agreement among Gaia DR3 analyses.
Finally, 790 periods appear in none of the sources searched, including SsODNet. An estimated 4--5\% of
them are noise and 61--87\% are correct; most of the rest are probably harmonics or aliases.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
The catalogue \citep{Tropynin2026b} (41{,}842 rows with periods, probabilities, tiers, second-best periods
and spin senses), the list of 790 candidate new periods with the prior periods found for the other 152, the
117 disputed LCDB periods with their supporting catalogues, the validation tables and the full code are
openly available on Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23638281, and at
\url{https://github.com/olegtropinin/gaia-dr3-rotation-catalog}.
